## Legal & IP Summary

All identified solutions are proprietary commercial software with closed-source code. No significant patent landscapes identified beyond normal SaaS business method patents. Most solutions implement standard OAuth 2.0, TLS encryption, and follow GDPR/CCPA compliance practices. No GPL or copyleft licence dependencies noted in public documentation. Pipedrive's 500+ integrations marketplace offers potential interoperability advantages but remains proprietary.
